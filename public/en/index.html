Milling machine failure detection with Random Forest

Failure detection for milling machines where only 3.4% of cases are failures. I compared logistic regression, SVM and Random Forest: the first two caught more failures, but at 14% and 27% precision they raised too many false alarms. Random Forest struck the best balance: 75% recall and 58% precision (F1 of 0.65).

  • Python
  • scikit-learn (Random Forest, SVM, logistic regression)
  • GridSearchCV and bootstrapping
